# Nova N4 Datasheet
Document: DS-NOVA-N4 Rev B

## Overview
The Nova N4 is a 4 TB, 3.5-inch, air-filled SATA hard drive. Entry-level drive for small servers, NAS and surveillance recorders.

## Specifications
- Capacity: 4 TB
- Interface: SATA 6 Gb/s
- Spindle speed: 5400 RPM
- Cache: 64 MB
- Sustained transfer rate: up to 150 MB/s
- Form factor: 3.5-inch
- Construction: air-filled

## Reliability
- MTBF: 1,000,000 hours
- Annualized failure rate (AFR): 0.87%
- Workload rate: 180 TB/year
- Non-recoverable read errors per bits read: 1 in 10^15

## Power and Environmental
- Idle power: 3.4 W
- Active (random read/write) power: 5.0 W
- Operating temperature: 5 °C to 55 °C
- Non-operating temperature: -40 °C to 70 °C
- Operating shock (2 ms): 70 G

## Warranty
The Nova N4 carries a limited warranty of 24 months (2 years) from the date of
purchase. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use within the
rated workload of 180 TB/year. Drives operated above the rated workload or outside the
operating temperature range may be excluded from warranty coverage.

## Support
Firmware updates and replacement requests are handled through the enterprise support portal.
Advance replacement is available for the duration of the warranty period.
