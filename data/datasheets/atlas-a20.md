# Atlas A20 Datasheet
Document: DS-ATLAS-A20 Rev B

## Overview
The Atlas A20 is a 20 TB, 3.5-inch, helium-sealed SATA hard drive. Helium-sealed high-capacity drive for hyperscale and data-lake deployments.

## Specifications
- Capacity: 20 TB
- Interface: SATA 6 Gb/s
- Spindle speed: 7200 RPM
- Cache: 512 MB
- Sustained transfer rate: up to 285 MB/s
- Form factor: 3.5-inch
- Construction: helium-sealed (helium)

## Reliability
- MTBF: 2,500,000 hours
- Annualized failure rate (AFR): 0.35%
- Workload rate: 550 TB/year
- Non-recoverable read errors per bits read: 1 in 10^15

## Power and Environmental
- Idle power: 4.6 W
- Active (random read/write) power: 7.4 W
- Operating temperature: 5 °C to 65 °C
- Non-operating temperature: -40 °C to 70 °C
- Operating shock (2 ms): 70 G

## Warranty
The Atlas A20 carries a limited warranty of 60 months (5 years) from the date of
purchase. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use within the
rated workload of 550 TB/year. Drives operated above the rated workload or outside the
operating temperature range may be excluded from warranty coverage.

## Support
Firmware updates and replacement requests are handled through the enterprise support portal.
Advance replacement is available for the duration of the warranty period.
